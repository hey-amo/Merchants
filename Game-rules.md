# Merchants - Game rules

## About

This is an unoffical creation of Reiner Kniza's game "Merchants"

The 16th century saw a boom in sailing, exploration and sea-based trading.

Players act as a 16th century merchant family and try to maximise profits through the exchange of good cubes that match good cards played to a market.

## Components

6 different goods:
:sugar (White)
:indigo (Blue)
:spices (Red)
:tea (Green)
:silk (Purple)
:lumber (Brown)

60 good cards
 - 10x Sugar, 10x Indigo, 10x Spices, 10x Tea, 10x Silk, 10x Lumber

30 good cubes in the 6 different goods
- 5x Sugar, 5x Indigo, 5x Spices, 5x Tea, 5x Silk, 5x Lumber

20 Special cards:
- 14 ship cards
- 2 office cards
- 2 docker cards
- 2 trade agreement cards



> Important rule: Each ship may only ever hold 1 cube.

## Game Preperation

1. Shuffle all 60 good cards and put face down forming a draw pile.
2. Deal 3 good cards to each player.  These cards are private to that player.
3. Draw 6 cards from the goods card draw pile and create a 2x3 face up into a `marketplace`.  This `marketplace` represents the current demand.
4. Give each player 2 ship cards, these are face up in front the respective player.
5. Create a common supply. Place all good cubes, coins, workers and separate the special cards into their categories, forming a common supply next to the game board.
6. Select a first player at random.

### Pre-start

Prior to game start, each player will now pick 1 cube to load each of their ships.

#### Snake draft cubes

Cubes are now drafted using a snake order until all players have exactly two goods cubes.

Starting with the last player, and going counter clockwise, each player now selects one available goods cube from the supply until the first player takes one goods cube and loads it onto one of their empty ships.  Then, the order reverses and now, going clockwise and starting from the first player, each player now selects their second available goods cube to load onto of one their empty ships.

*Important: Each ship can only hold 1 goods cube*

## Sequence of play

The game is played over a series of rounds until the market draw deck exhausts. The active player completes both phases and ends their turn before the game checks for an empty draw deck. If the deck is empty, the game ends before the next player starts a turn.

In a round, a player will take a single `turn`.

Play always proceeds clockwise from the First player.

Starting with the First player, and continuing clockwise, each player will become the active player (on turn), and MUST perform each game phase (Purchase, Deliver), in sequential order. After the active player completes Phase 2 and ends their turn, check the end-game condition; only if the game continues does play move clockwise to the next player.

*Important: The first player never changes*


### Phase 1: Purchase

The active player MUST only choose 1 action in Phase 1.

- Exchange 1 goods cube OR,
- Buy 1 special card OR,
- Pass (do nothing)

### Phase 2: Deliver

The active player MUST either:

- Deliver: Playing good cards, and trigger scoring
- Draw 2 new good cards

Note: A player cannot delivery and draw 2 goods cards, they must choose.

--

### Exchanging goods cube

A player may exchange ONE goods cube from ONE of his ships with a different colour from the supply, if its available.

### Buy a special card

The player may spend coins to purchase a single special card, if its available and adds it in front of them (adding it to their tableau).  The effects of special cards are cumulative.

### Deliver: Playing good cards and trigger scoring

The player may play any number of good cards of the SAME colour from his hand onto the goods cards in the centre of the table, thus changing the marketplace's demand.

The marketplace is always 6 cards.

Cards played MUST played on top of existing cards in the marketplace, and may never play cards to the same market index in the same delivery action.

*Important: Each card must be played on top of a different card within the marketplace.**

#### Triggering scoring

When cards are `delivered` to the marketplace, it triggers scoring, and *all* players get paid out, dependent upon the colour good cubes they have on their ships.

Every player with the same colour goods cube on their ships gets 1 coin per goods cube per matching goods card in the marketplace.

*Example: The marketplace currently shows 1 Green, 2 Yellow, 1 Blue, 1 Red, 1 Brown.  Tom, plays 2 blue cards from his hand and places them on top of 1 Red, 1 Brown respectively, and now the marketplace shows 3 blue cards.  All players get 3 coins for each blue goods cube located on their ships.*

### Drawing two new good cards

The active player draws two new gods cards, adding them to their hand.

*Important: There is a hand limit of 6 cards*

## Special cards

## Ship (cost: 10 coins)
Each ship carrys one goods cube. After purchasing a ship, they must load it with an available goods cube.

If no goods cube exists, no ships may be purchased

### Office (cost: 8 coins)
At the end of phase 2, the player may draw a new goods card, regardless of which action they took in Phase 2

### Docker (cost: 12 coins)
The player may exchange an extra goods cube in Phase 1, regardless of the action chosen.  This may be performed as soon as this card has been purchased.

## Trade agreement (cost: 11 coins)
When a delivery occurs by the owner of this card, they receive 2 coins

> Example: The owner of two "trade agreement" special cards will receive four additional gold coins each time he scores instead of two.

# Game end

After the active player completes their turn, the game ends if the card draw deck is empty. The player with the most coins wins.

Special cards purchased during the game do not count towards victory.
